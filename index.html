Questions brands ask first

Who owns the videos?

You do. Every delivery comes with full commercial usage rights, and we only use tools whose terms allow commercial use of generated output.

Will it look like AI?

Not if we do our job. A human director reviews every frame, fixes artefacts by hand and grades everything to your brand palette before you see it.

Do you label AI-generated content?

Yes, wherever a platform or law requires it. We set up the right disclosure labels for Meta, TikTok and YouTube as part of delivery.

Is it safe for my brand?

We never generate real people's likenesses, competitors' products or copyrighted characters. Your brand book becomes a checklist every video passes before delivery.

How many rounds of edits do I get?

One on Test, two on Launch, and unlimited small edits on Always-on. Changing the core idea after the storyboard counts as a new video.

What do you need from us?

Your product, your brand book if you have one, and 30 minutes for the brief. Product photos help but are not required.
